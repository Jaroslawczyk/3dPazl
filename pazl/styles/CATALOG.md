# Style catalog / roadmap

Implemented (YAML + macros): generic, greek, roman, romanesque, gothic, renaissance, baroque, classicism,
russian_orthodox, russian_wooden, lithuanian, german_fachwerk, medieval_castle, dugout, soviet_panel,
stalinist, constructivism, modern.

Next packs (same pattern: YAML with materials/defaults/tips + optional macro + missing generators):
- Ancient: egyptian (pylons, obelisks, hypostyle halls), mesopotamian (ziggurat), persian, minoan, etruscan.
- Byzantine (cross-in-square, pendentive domes), armenian/georgian (conical drums), bulgarian/serbian.
- Islamic: ottoman (pencil minarets, cascading domes), persian (iwans, muqarnas, tile domes), moorish (horseshoe arches),
  mughal (onion domes, chhatris), central asian.
- Asian: chinese (dougong, glazed roofs, pagodas), japanese (shoin, pagoda, torii), korean (hanok), thai (prang),
  khmer, indian hindu temples (shikhara, gopuram), tibetan.
- Americas: maya/aztec step pyramids, pueblo adobe, colonial spanish, american craftsman, victorian, art deco,
  log cabin (north american), prairie style.
- Europe more: norman, scandinavian stave church, tudor, georgian, dutch gabled houses, swiss chalet, polish/ukrainian
  wooden churches, gothic revival, art nouveau / modern (stil modern), neo-russian (pseudo-russian style).
- 20th c.: bauhaus, brutalism, soviet modernism (mosaics, bus stops), post-modern, deconstructivism, hi-tech,
  industrial (factories, water towers, hangars), military (bunkers, pillboxes, fortifications), infrastructure
  (bridges, stations, lighthouses, windmills, watermills).
- Fantasy & sci-fi: elven, dwarven, steampunk, cyberpunk megablocks, space colonies.
